What's in them

Grown, not drilled.

Most sneakers are made from oil: polyester uppers, EVA foam, synthetic rubber. We swapped each part for something that grows back.

Upper

Hemp canvas

Hemp grows fast with little water and no pesticides. The canvas is tough, breathes well, and gets softer the more you wear it.

Sole

Natural rubber

Tapped from rubber trees on FSC-certified plantations. We blend it with sugarcane-based foam for bounce, so there's no petroleum EVA.

Footbed

Portuguese cork

Harvested from the bark of cork oaks without cutting the tree down. It cushions, wicks moisture, and fights odor on its own.

Laces & thread

Recycled PET

Each pair of laces uses about one plastic bottle. It's the one synthetic part, because hemp laces fray too fast.

Take-back

Done with them? Send them home.

The worst part of any shoe is the landfill at the end. So we pay for the trip back and give you €20 off your next pair.

  1. Print a free label

    Any Loams, any condition. Holes and dog chews included. Drop the box at a post office.

  2. We sort and grind

    Wearable pairs get cleaned and donated. The rest is split up: rubber is ground into crumb, cork into granules, hemp into fiber.

  3. It becomes new soles

    Up to 30% of every new Loam sole is old Loam soles. Your €20 code lands in your inbox once the box arrives.

Questions

The stuff people ask.

Are they waterproof?

Water-resistant, not waterproof. The waxed hemp on Canopy handles puddles and wet grass. None of them are for wading through rivers.

How long do they last?

Most people get two to three years of daily wear. Field and Canopy use stitched cupsoles, so a cobbler can resole them instead of you buying a new pair.

Can I machine wash them?

Take out the cork insole and laces, then wash the shoes cold on a gentle cycle. Let them air dry. The dryer will shrink the hemp.

Are they vegan?

Yes. No leather, no animal glue, no wool. The glue is water-based.
